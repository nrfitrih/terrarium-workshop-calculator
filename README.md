# Terrarium Palace Workshop Calculator

A public workshop price calculator by **Terrarium Palace**, designed to help clients estimate the investment required for terrarium and plant-based workshops.

## About Terrarium Palace

Terrarium Palace is a creative nature experience brand based in Bogor, Indonesia, established in 2018.

**Create a Terrarium, Calm Your Mind.**

## Calculator Features

- Select workshop types.
- Estimate workshop kit costs.
- Set the number of participants and kits.
- Choose event location.
- Calculate facilitator and assistant fees.
- Add transportation and shipping costs.
- Include additional event expenses.
- View estimated total investment and payment breakdown.

## How to Use

1. Open the published calculator website.
2. Select your workshop type.
3. Enter the number of participants and kits.
4. Select your event location.
5. Add shipping, transportation, and other costs where applicable.
6. Review your estimated workshop investment.

## Important Notes

- All calculations are preliminary estimates, not final quotations.
- Shipping and certain transportation costs depend on the actual destination and operational requirements.
- Events involving more than 60 participants require a customized quotation.
- Final prices and arrangements must be confirmed directly with Terrarium Palace.
